Privacy policy

POD WAWONA Private Backup is a personal backup utility used by its owner. This policy describes that utility's Google Drive connection.

Access and storage

The utility uses Google OAuth and the limited drive.file permission to create and access specific files used with this app. Backup contents are encrypted before transfer. Encrypted archives, recovery manifests and verification records are stored in the owner's private Google Drive folder.

Access credentials are stored in restricted local configuration files. Any recovery backup of credentials is encrypted. The recovery private key is kept separately and is not uploaded with the archives.

Use of data

The utility uses files and verification information only to create backups, check their integrity and recover the owner's application data. It does not sell that data, use it for advertising or operate a public artwork-sharing service. Google processes API requests and stores files under its own terms and privacy policy.

Retention and control

The owner controls backup retention and may remove backup files from Google Drive. The owner may revoke this app's authorization through Google Account connections. Revoking authorization stops future access; it does not automatically delete existing backup files.

This site provides information about the utility and does not collect account credentials or provide a login form.
